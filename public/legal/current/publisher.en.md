# NexaStore Publisher Distribution Agreement

**Version: 1.0**  
**Effective: September 26, 2026**

English translation of the original Chinese agreement, available from the legal documents page.

This Agreement applies to individuals and organizations uploading, submitting, publishing or maintaining plugins and other digital resources through NexaStore. NexaStore is operated by **Xie Jiangmao (谢江懋)**, an individual operating as **Nexa / PCL N**.

It supplements the Nexa Cloud Terms of Service. Its specific provisions prevail for publisher uploads, review and distribution where they conflict; mandatory law takes priority.

## 1. Scope

This Agreement covers publisher profiles and organizations, namespace applications, projects, uploads, version review, free publishing, maintenance, updates and removal. NexaStore v1.0 initially supports third-party **free resources** only. Paid third-party sales, developer settlements, commercial DRM and Chargeback Protection are not provided until separately enabled and require corresponding commercial agreements.

## 2. Eligibility

Publishers may be individuals or lawfully established organizations. Representatives must have authorization. For security, compliance and operational capacity, Nexa may use invitations, limit applications, organization projects, uploads or pending versions, or pause submissions. These restrictions do not permit removing existing lawful rights without reasons.

## 3. Identity and organizations

Projects must belong to an identified publisher or organization. Do not impersonate developers, organizations or official projects, misuse trademarks, invent partnerships/authorizations or deceive through confusing names. Submitters of collaborative projects must hold the necessary authority to publish on their behalf.

## 4. Rights in content

Publishers retain their lawful existing rights in code, assets, images, documentation, names and trademarks. You confirm you have upload/distribution rights, have obtained necessary third-party permissions, and will not intentionally remove notices required by law or license. Access to a file is not redistribution permission. Dependencies that users may download but publishers or Nexa cannot redistribute **must not be bundled in NexaStore-hosted packages**.

## 5. Licenses

Every public resource needs a clear license. Presets may include MIT, Apache License 2.0, GPL-3.0, LGPL-3.0, MPL-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, All Rights Reserved and Custom. Comply with applicable source, notice, redistribution and other open-source requirements. All Rights Reserved grants no rights beyond law. Custom licenses require full text, may include a website/URL, and must not violate mandatory law. Changes generally apply to later expressly released versions and must not retrospectively remove rights by editing a page.

## 6. Distribution permission

You grant Nexa a nonexclusive permission necessary to receive/store uploads, hash, unpack/check structure, analyze metadata/permissions, scan, preview, review manually or automatically, verify publisher signatures, attach Nexa marketplace signatures/manifests to approved artifacts, cache, deliver through CDN/infrastructure to lawful users, and retain necessary security, dispute and audit evidence. Ownership does not transfer. This does not authorize arbitrary sales, independent commercialization or general-purpose AI training with private content or nonpublic source code.

## 7. Upload requirements

Meet supported formats and resource types. Do not upload files without distribution rights, malware, backdoors, credential theft, hidden miners, undisclosed malicious remote control, security-bypass code, deceptive data collection or other content prohibited by the NexaStore Acceptable Use and Content Policy. Nexa may limit upload size, file count, expanded size, compression ratio, paths, nesting and formats, and reject technical violations before review.

## 8. Permissions and data declarations

Truthfully declare access to user data, network, files, accounts, cloud or sensitive capabilities. Do not conceal access under vague names, add undisclosed processing after review, indirectly acquire prohibited data, induce disclosure of passwords/tokens/secrets or read Microsoft/Xbox/Minecraft/Nexa internal credentials as ordinary plugin data. Independent processors must provide their own legally required privacy notices; Nexa's Policy does not replace their duties.

## 9. Versions and immutability

Each version needs a unique valid ID. After approval and release, do not replace artifacts in place, secretly alter a program under the same version identity or replace signed files while claiming the original approval. Submit new versions for fixes or actual changes. Nexa may require strictly increasing supported version numbers.

## 10. Review

Checks may cover format, integrity, permissions, dependencies, security scans, signatures, automated rules and manual review. Outcomes may be approval, requested changes, rejection, quarantine or requests for information. Approval is tied to artifact hash, metadata, permissions, license and review-rule version, and may become invalid when those change. Review/signing does not guarantee freedom from vulnerabilities or perpetual compatibility.

## 11. Release

Approved resources may be published or scheduled through supported methods. Release requires complete artifacts, completed checks, valid current approval, publisher eligibility, valid license, authorized operator and no security revocation. Approval does not guarantee immediate public release.

## 12. Downloads and distribution

Nexa may use R2, CDN, temporary authorizations, signed URLs, chunked downloads and other reasonable delivery methods. Publishers cannot require disclosure of storage credentials, signing keys, internal addresses or other users' authorizations. Public downloads, account acquisitions and restricted entitlements may use different delivery methods.

## 13. Ordinary removal

Publishers may request removal. It immediately stops new public distribution/acquisition; pages may be hidden or unavailable. Lawfully held local copies do not disappear, and their licenses determine continued use. Normal superseded artifacts may be deleted after a technical rollback period of up to about 30 days. Hashes, signatures, reviews, security records and necessary audits may remain under retention policies.

## 14. Security revocation

Reasonable evidence of malicious activity, serious vulnerabilities, tampering, leaked publisher keys, signature anomalies, major deception or other real user security risks allows immediate revocation. Measures may stop all new downloads and updates, revoke distribution permission, stop future DRM key issuance, and block protected resources on the next online security check in supported clients. Ordinary-removal grace rules do not apply. Reasons and appeals should be provided without jeopardizing investigation or user security.

## 15. Account deletion

Before deletion, handle members, projects, pending versions, public resources and unresolved disputes where possible. Relationships that cannot be removed directly may transfer to a system custodian unable to sign in or conduct business. Resources immediately leave the public store, with no new releases, license/price changes or sales. Necessary records remain only for integrity, security, rights evidence and disputes.

## 16. Storage is not source backup

Publishing storage primarily supports distribution. Keep your own source, build environments, signing keys, assets and long-term release copies. Nexa does not promise permanent storage of every historical version as your sole backup.

## 17. Reports and intellectual property

Report suspected infringement to `legal@pcln.top` and security issues to `security@pcln.top`. Nexa may request rights-holder identity, content, legal basis and evidence sufficient to identify the complaint. Where lawful, necessary information may be forwarded to the publisher to allow a response.

## 18. Violations

Depending on nature/risk, Nexa may warn, request correction, reject review, pause submissions, restrict releases, remove resources, revoke for security, suspend eligibility or, in serious cases, terminate relevant capabilities. Decisions consider conduct, risk, intent, repetition, timely correction and user impact. Significant effects on rights receive an appropriate appeal channel.

## 19. Rule updates

Law, security and platform development may require revisions, with prominent notice for important changes. If future Marketplace trading makes NexaStore a regulated online trading platform, rule creation/modification, archive retention and merchant feedback follow applicable law. Future revisions do not arbitrarily rewrite acquired licenses or past conduct.
