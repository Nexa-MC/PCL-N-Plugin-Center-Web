Monthly/yearly Cloud+ plans may renew automatically. Manage billing through your Nexa account. Paddle subscriptions may link to the Customer Portal or use supported cancellation APIs. Paddle subscription emails may also provide cancellation. No additional purchase is required to cancel.

Eligible users may receive one seven-day Lite trial. A valid payment method is required, with trial end, subsequent charge/period and automatic paid conversion clearly disclosed. Cancel before the first charge to avoid it. Otherwise billing starts automatically on the confirmed plan after the trial.

## 6. Preventing repeat trials

Generally one trial per eligible person. Account, payment identity, trial history and necessary anti-fraud signals may be used in a limited way. This does not grant arbitrary long-term fingerprinting or cross-service tracking rights. Clear bulk registration, impersonation or other abuse may cancel eligibility.

## 7. Failed payments

Failed renewal may enter a **payment grace period of up to three days**. Nexa may request payment updates, restrict new high-cost resources and temporarily retain subscription data. Continued failure afterward downgrades to an available lower or free plan. Safe retry behavior/count depends on the actual provider; an external timeout alone does not cause Nexa to create duplicate payments.

## 8. Storage downgrade

### 8.1 Immediate restrictions

If data exceeds the new capacity, uploads and storage-increasing changes stop, while deletion remains available.

### 8.2 Readable grace period

An additional **seven-day over-quota grace period** uses a deterministic readable-file set. Generally files sort by increasing size, ties use stable order, and the cumulative selected size stays within the plan. Selected files can be read; other excess files temporarily cannot. If every individual file exceeds total capacity, the set may be empty.

### 8.3 After grace

Still-over-quota accounts become **OverQuotaLocked** after seven days. A downgrade alone does not automatically delete files. Deletion, additional storage or upgrading can restore access.

## 29. N Token top-ups and refunds of unused portions

The top-up ratio is **CNY 1 = 10 N Tokens**. Customer balances, product prices and requested token refunds use whole tokens; fractional tokens are not offered.

Only the **unused portion of an original top-up** may be requested for refund. Eligibility is checked against the original top-up lot, consumption allocations, prior refunds and tokens reserved by other requests. Spent tokens are not automatically restored as refundable balance.

Refunds require manual approval and return to the **original payment method and transaction**, not another recipient selected by the applicant. A request reserves the relevant tokens to prevent duplicate refunds or concurrent spending. Application, approval, provider execution and receipt of funds are separate stages; a submitted or approved request does not mean the refund has been paid.

Where applicable law and the actual transaction provider's rules permit, the refund deducts verifiable **payment-provider fees or commission** attributable to the original transaction. This is separate from the marketplace's 20% platform revenue share; that share must not be substituted for actual payment fees. Any applicable right to a full refund under law or provider rules is preserved.

Whether requesting all or part of the unused tokens, the refund applicant bears the full actual payment-provider fee verified for the original top-up transaction; it is not prorated by the requested refund amount. This fee is allocated and deducted only once per original transaction. Approval reserves the fee allocation, and later requests must not deduct the same fee again. Approval and reservation do not mean an actual refund has been executed.

If the requested gross refund is smaller than the full fee still due, no negative net refund is created and no difference is automatically charged to another top-up or account. The request must be adjusted or reviewed manually. Unknown or changed actual fees and disputed transactions require verification before approval; an unknown fee is not treated as zero.

This revision adds no universal application deadline, review duration or time-to-receipt promise. Mandatory requirements of applicable law or the actual provider still apply. Manual review identifies the verified original transaction, gross amount, applicable deduction and expected refund; unavailable actual refund execution is clearly identified.

Reference: [Paddle Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) for transactions actually sold through Paddle. Legal applicability and provider terms must be checked for the actual transaction, region and current rules; this Policy gives no unconditional assurance of legal compliance.

## 30. Author account deletion and unpaid income

Before requesting account deletion, authors must be shown their current total unpaid store income, including withdrawal requests awaiting review and approved withdrawals awaiting payment, and separately confirm that these unpaid amounts will not be withdrawn or paid and will be forfeited after confirmation, the existing 7-day cooling-off period and completed deletion. Additional income accrued but still unpaid during that period is also covered. Pending and approved unpaid withdrawals are included in the total, not added twice.

Cancelling deletion during the cooling-off period cancels this deletion disposition. Unverifiable amounts must not be presented as zero, and historical author income without valid confirmation is not automatically forfeited. Necessary confirmation and audit records are retained. This description does not claim that any real user deletion, payment or removal of funds has been executed. Mandatory statutory rights and obligations remain unaffected.
