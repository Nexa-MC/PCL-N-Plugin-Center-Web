# Nexa Refund, Trial and Subscription Policy

**Version: 1.0**  
**Effective: September 26, 2026**

English translation of the original Chinese policy, available from the legal documents page.

This Policy covers directly supplied paid digital products/services, including Cloud+, cloud storage, AI allowances and other clearly identified Nexa products when available. It excludes the unopened paid third-party NexaStore Marketplace, which will require separate trading and developer commercial rules.

Nexa remains responsible for the product, technical service and purchase-page commitments.

## 2. Pricing

Before purchase, show product/plan, price, currency, billing period, renewal, trial, first charge date and cancellation method. Nexa generally uses **tax-inclusive prices**. Where regional or seller rules differ, the final checkout governs.

## 3. Automatic renewal

Monthly/yearly Cloud+ plans may renew automatically. Confirmation authorizes the actual transaction provider to charge at the displayed interval until cancellation, termination, continued payment failure or product discontinuation. Cancellation prevents new renewal orders and ordinarily takes effect at the end of the paid period, except immediate cancellation, refunds or legal requirements.

## 4. Cancellation

Manage billing through your Nexa account. Paddle subscriptions may link to the Customer Portal or use supported cancellation APIs. Paddle subscription emails may also provide cancellation. No additional purchase is required to cancel.

## 5. Lite seven-day trial

Eligible users may receive one seven-day Lite trial. A valid payment method is required, with trial end, subsequent charge/period and automatic paid conversion clearly disclosed. Cancel before the first charge to avoid it. Otherwise billing starts automatically on the confirmed plan after the trial.

## 6. Preventing repeat trials

Generally one trial per eligible person. Account, payment identity, trial history and necessary anti-fraud signals may be used in a limited way. This does not grant arbitrary long-term fingerprinting or cross-service tracking rights. Clear bulk registration, impersonation or other abuse may cancel eligibility.

## 7. Failed payments

Failed renewal may enter a **payment grace period of up to three days**. Nexa may request payment updates, restrict new high-cost resources and temporarily retain subscription data. Continued failure afterward downgrades to an available lower or free plan. Safe retry behavior/count depends on the actual provider; an external timeout alone does not cause Nexa to create duplicate payments.

## 8. Storage downgrade

### 8.1 Immediate restrictions

If data exceeds the new capacity, uploads and storage-increasing changes stop, while deletion remains available.

### 8.2 Readable grace period

An additional **seven-day over-quota grace period** uses a deterministic readable-file set. Generally files sort by increasing size, ties use stable order, and the cumulative selected size stays within the plan. Selected files can be read; other excess files temporarily cannot. If every individual file exceeds total capacity, the set may be empty.

### 8.3 After grace

Still-over-quota accounts become **OverQuotaLocked** after seven days. A downgrade alone does not automatically delete files. Deletion, additional storage or upgrading can restore access.

## 9. General refunds

Duplicate charges, paid non-delivery, Nexa/transaction-system errors, material discrepancies from purchase descriptions, promised core paid functions unavailable because of Nexa and not reasonably repairable, and legally required refunds/withdrawal/remedies should normally receive priority investigation and refunds, redelivery or other reasonable remedies.

## 10. Change of mind

Except where law, purchase pages or express commitments say otherwise, successfully delivered and usable digital services are not guaranteed a refund solely for a change of mind. Requests may still be reviewed. Paddle transactions also follow its then-current refund policy. Non-waivable consumer rights are not excluded.

## 11. Current billing period

Ordinary cancellation usually allows use through the paid period and does not automatically prorate unused time. Statutory withdrawal, major outages, duplicate charges or Nexa's express commitments are handled under their applicable rules.

## 12. First charge after trial

If a clearly disclosed Lite trial converts to paid, request a refund through Nexa or Paddle support where applicable. Decisions consider law, actual use and current transaction rules. Hidden or unclear renewal notices are not grounds to reject reasonable refunds.

## 13. Renewal price changes

Appropriate notice precedes future charges at new prices. Where law/provider requires renewed agreement, no new-price charge occurs without authorization. Completed historical transactions are not recalculated after future pricing changes.

## 14. Upgrades

New capacity/features activate after successful verified payment. Proration follows the purchase page and actual provider. A browser “payment successful” message alone does not grant permanent unverified benefits.

## 15. Downgrades

Downgrades normally take effect at period end or another explicitly chosen supported time, shown before confirmation. Storage consequences follow section 8.

## 16. AI allowances

Plan allowances and separately purchased extras should retain separate sources. Expiring a plan must not incorrectly remove valid independent extras. Consumed computing resources generally cannot become unused again; failed services without the corresponding result may restore allowances according to actual metering. Units and expiry follow the purchase page.

## 17. Additional storage

Storage may be included in Cloud+ or bought separately. Valid entitlements from different sources should be recorded separately. Canceling one subscription must not incorrectly revoke other independent capacity.

## 18. Requesting refunds

Use `support@pcln.top` or account order/support controls. Provide account, order ID, product, problem description and relevant errors where needed. **Do not send full card numbers, CVV, passwords or unnecessary payment credentials.** Paddle sales may also use Paddle buyer support.

## 19. Processing refunds

Approved refunds for Paddle sales use supported Paddle refund mechanisms, not private replacement transfers. Request status differs from actual receipt. We distinguish Requested, Approved, Processing, Pending Verification, Completed and Failed. Funds are not reported received before the external payment institution confirms success.

## 20. Partial refunds

Where supported, eligible orders may receive full or partial refunds. Amounts must be reconciled against the order, prior refunds and rules; cumulative refunds cannot exceed actual payment.

## 21. Chargebacks

Lawful payment disputes/chargebacks do not automatically ban accounts. Reasonable evidence of fraud, malicious chargebacks, deliberate denial after repeated acquisition or systematic refund abuse may restrict disputed benefits during investigation. Lawful consumer disputes and malicious fraud are handled separately.

## 22. Benefits after refunds

Completed refunds may revoke entitlements from **that order**. Independent entitlements from other orders, subscriptions, compensation, redemption or lawful sources must remain.

## 23. Discontinuation

If Nexa voluntarily ends an important paid service permanently and cannot supply the paid remainder, migration, alternatives, refunds or other reasonable remedies may follow the remaining period, delivered content and law.

## 24. Regional limits

During v1.0 Nexa does not actively offer paid services to the EEA or UK. If technical/configuration errors accept a restricted-region transaction, that restriction does not remove legally acquired mandatory consumer rights.

## 25. Paddle terms

Paddle Buyer Terms, Refund Policy and other checkout conditions also apply to its sales. More favorable non-waivable rights under Paddle or law prevail.

## 26. Updates

Product plans, providers, law, trials or plan structures may require revisions. Completed orders retain core commercial conditions applicable at confirmation unless law requires otherwise or new rules favor the user. Important changes receive reasonable notice.
